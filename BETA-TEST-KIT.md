# Meraki Companion Beta Test Kit

**[Download the Meraki Companion 0.4.60 Beta 2 Test Kit (ZIP)](https://github.com/RotaryPumpers/Meraki-Companion-Preview/releases/download/v0.4.60-beta.2/RP-Meraki-Companion-0.4.60-Beta.2-Test-Kit.zip)** · [Prerelease notes and warnings](https://github.com/RotaryPumpers/Meraki-Companion-Preview/releases/tag/v0.4.60-beta.2)

The ZIP includes the Companion Android APK and the Mac launcher for installing Secure Link, pairing your phone, rolling back, and removing the machine service. Extract it and open `START-HERE.md`.

This experimental prerelease is for the documented US 110 V machine profile with MCU 5.1.4 and a compatible display build. Fresh installation, rollback, and removal passed simulation. Live testing with this exact kit is pending.

Beta 2 replaces the withdrawn Beta 1 ZIP. Discard downloaded copies of Beta 1.
